MrNaanuYT

MrNaanuYT is a personal tool used by the owner of the MrNaanuYT YouTube channel to manage their own channel.

What it does

  • Shows channel and video statistics
  • Uploads videos and sets titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails
  • Reads and replies to comments on the channel's videos

It uses the YouTube Data API and only accesses the YouTube account that the owner signs in with. It is not offered to the public.
