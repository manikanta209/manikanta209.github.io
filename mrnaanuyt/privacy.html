Who we are

MrNaanuYT is a personal tool used only by the owner of the MrNaanuYT YouTube channel. Contact: rempire046@gmail.com.

What data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app uses the YouTube Data API to access the YouTube account you choose: channel and video details, statistics, comments, and the ability to upload and edit videos and reply to comments.

How data is used

Data is used only to show channel information and to perform the actions the owner requests (uploads, edits, comment replies). It is not used for advertising or profiling.

Storage

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No YouTube data is stored on any server or shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
